One 100 g box serves two people. That's it — that's the recipe.

  1. 1

    Cook your rice

    Any rice works. Let it cool slightly so the grains stay separate.

What goes in the kadai

Real ingredients you can pronounce

Every batch is roasted, ground and cooked down by hand until the oil floats — the old way.

Tamarind

Squeezed by hand for that deep, puckery sourness.

Pure jaggery

Never white sugar. Rounds off the tang with a mellow sweetness.

Dried red chillies

Fried in oil for heat that builds, Andhra style.

Chana & urad dal

Roasted golden, then ground with coriander seeds.

Sesame seeds

Toasted for the nutty aroma that makes it pulihora.

Groundnut tadka

Peanuts, mustard and chana dal crackled in oil. Plus rock salt.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How much gojju do I use?

About two spoons per cup of cooked rice. A 100 g box makes enough pulihora for two people. Adjust to taste — some like it extra tangy.

How should I store it?

Always use a clean, dry spoon and keep it refrigerated after opening. The layer of oil on top protects the gojju — don't stir it away.

Is it very spicy?

It's Andhra style, so it has a proper kick balanced by tamarind and jaggery. Tell us when you order if you'd like it milder and we'll let you know what's possible for the next batch.

Does it contain allergens?

Yes — it contains peanuts (groundnuts), sesame and mustard. It's 100% vegetarian.

How do I order and pay?

Message or call us on WhatsApp with the box size. We cook fresh every week and will confirm your pickup time. Pickup is from our home kitchen in Bengaluru.
